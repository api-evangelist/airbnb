To support those impacted by several active wildfires in Colorado, Airbnb.org—a nonprofit founded by Airbnb to provide emergency housing in times of crisis—is working with Mile High United Way and its 211 Colorado statewide Help Line to offer free, emergency housing to displaced residents and first responders. Stays are free for guests and are funded by Airbnb.org and the generosity of donors.
