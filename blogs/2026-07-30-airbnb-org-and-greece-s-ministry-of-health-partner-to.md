Airbnb.org, a nonprofit founded by Airbnb to provide emergency housing in times of crisis, and Greece's Ministry of Health announced a new pilot partnership to provide free stays for doctors and nurses working at Public Health Centers on five Greek islands during the high-demand summer season, when local housing shortages make it difficult for essential medical staff to find affordable places to live near their posts.
